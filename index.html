Quick Help

Browse Businesses

  • Use "Explore" to see all local businesses
  • Use category filters to narrow by type
  • Smart search shows suggestions as you type
  • Press / anywhere to focus the search bar

Reviews & Ratings

  • Click "Review" on any business card
  • Rate 1–5 stars and write at least 20 characters
  • Complete the "I'm not a robot" check
  • Like/dislike others' reviews to help the community

Deals & Coupons

  • Go to the Deals tab for all promotions
  • Click "Coupon" on any card for a printable coupon

Assistant

  • Ask about any business, category, or deal
  • Get smart recommendations based on your favourites

Accessibility

  • Full keyboard navigation throughout
  • Press Esc to close any modal
  • All controls have screen reader labels
  • Toggle dark mode for reduced eye strain

Analytics & Export

  • Filter and sort before exporting a custom JSON report
  • Compare any two businesses side-by-side
